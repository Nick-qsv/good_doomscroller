# Requested books for this release

This historical review artifact selected **15 quotations from each of seven
requested books**, a proposed addition of **105 quotations** to its 213-item
baseline. A subsequent [additive batch](../corpus/published/notes/daily-additions-2026-09-13.md)
prepared another 21 quotations, and the later [12-book expansion](twelve-book-expansion-2026-09-13.md)
proposed 180 more. Those proposals were superseded by policy review; their
retired editions were not published. The current live catalog instead contains
**265 passages across 14 books**, including the separately reviewed
[60-passage science expansion](../corpus/published/notes/science-expansion-2026-09-13.md).
Selection and a second, independent review of this historical proposal were
performed by assistants; no human editorial approval is claimed.

Every source below was checked against its official Project Gutenberg catalog
on **2026-09-13 UTC**. Each linked record classifies that specific English ebook
as **“Public domain in the USA.”** This records the provider's edition-specific
classification, including translation credits; it is not a worldwide rights
claim or a claim about a different edition. The original downloaded bytes,
retrieval metadata, source hashes, and complete normalized text are preserved
in each verification bundle.

## What was reviewed

Each selected passage is an exact, source-addressable sentence span. Review
covered complete thoughts, surrounding context, speaker attribution,
duplicates, and explanatory notes. The deterministic heuristic scores are
preserved as scores; they are not presented as editorial approval. Separate
AI context is visibly labeled and does not alter the quotation or its receipt.
Source reproduction and schema validation were run on the final bundles.
Edition-specific decisions and selected locations are recorded in
`corpus/published/notes/` alongside the release artifacts.

The normalizer intentionally retains the entire downloaded source, including
unselected introductions and notices. Retention in the verification bundle
does not mean those sections are selected as quotations. Exact-text checks
also do not establish the historical truth of a memoir or biography.

## Growing beyond this release

The [library growth workflow](library-growth.md) prepares additional review
queues from preserved books without a model API. Its earlier **280 staged
candidate windows** came from the original seven books. The later additive
batch selected some and revised others; the remaining rows are not approved.
Queue-window counts are separate from published quotation counts. New sources provide
additional authors and perspectives, while deeper selection can extend each
book over time. Candidate-window counts overlap and must never be reported
as distinct publication-ready quotations.

A longer-term target is **3,000 reviewed quotations from at least 30 varied
books**, not current inventory. Useful subsequent sources from the earlier
research list include [The Prophet](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/58585),
[The Tao Teh King, James Legge translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/216),
[The Story of My Life](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2397),
[Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11030),
[The Chemical History of a Candle](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14474),
[Twenty Years at Hull House](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1325),
[Wonderful Adventures of Mrs. Seacole in Many Lands](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/23031),
and [Sadhana](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6842). These remain **proposed**,
with no import or quotation yield claimed. Apply the same edition-specific
source and contextual review described in [Adding a book](adding-books.md).
