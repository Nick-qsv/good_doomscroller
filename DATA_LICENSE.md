# Corpus and data licensing

The application source code is licensed under the MIT License. The literary
works represented in the corpus are not covered by that software license.

Good Doomscroller only accepts editions that have been reviewed as usable in
the project's target jurisdiction. Every imported edition must retain its
source URL, retrieval timestamp, source digest, rights basis, and edition
metadata. Do not submit a text merely because its author is old or because a
copy is available online.

Project-authored manifests, rankings, tags, and other original corpus metadata
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal where legally
possible: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Quoted text remains subject to the legal status of its underlying work and
edition. Contributors are responsible for completing the rights fields in the
book manifest and for following the source provider's terms.

## Conservative publication review

The active library is limited to the exact editions and preserved source hashes
in `apps/web/scripts/publication-policy.json`. A U.S. public-domain label alone
does not authorize publication. The September 2026 screening requires an
identified historical edition published no later than 1930 and identified
relevant historical contributors who died no later than 1925, with an
applicable public-domain dedication or permission for supplemental digital
material. Unknown provenance or a later substantive contribution requires
withholding the source bundle pending a new review. This is a conservative
editorial threshold, not a worldwide legal clearance or a universal statement
of copyright duration. Local moral rights and other special rules may apply.

See [the review and retained library](docs/legal/copyright-review-2026-09-13.md)
and its edition-by-edition evidence. The importer and database reject
unapproved or retired editions; a new or changed source requires an explicit
review and corresponding policy migration before publication.

Project Gutenberg sources retain their original notices. The required notice
is displayed with the reading and verification views, and the full license is
available at `/source-license`, `/project-gutenberg-license.txt`, and in proof
downloads. The site offers these materials without charge. Any future paid
distribution or change in source attribution requires reviewing the
[Project Gutenberg license](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html)
before release. This project is not affiliated with Project Gutenberg.
