# Older-edition candidate shortlist, September 13, 2026

These are candidates for the next edition audit, not newly authorized feed sources.
No candidate below was imported or published during this search. Keep the existing
8-edition source-hash allowlist until each replacement/addition has its own audit.
An old underlying work does not clear a modern introduction, translation, artwork,
or the source provider's reuse terms. No worldwide clearance is claimed.

The strongest direction is an identified historical English edition with no new
translation. Prefer original-era page scans and a fresh transcription where the
available ebook has untraced additions. Verify the complete downloadable bundle,
not only the selected quote. Check every contributor, record the edition and
provider terms, preserve source bytes, and bind the eventual approval to hashes.

## Edition rejected during this search

[PG45109, The Enchiridion](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45109) uses Higginson's
translation but also has Albert Salomon's 1948 introduction, a 1955 second-edition
statement, and publisher revisions/notes. Its ancient author and old translation
do not qualify that complete file under our current conservative screen. Do not
add it from that file. An independently checked eighteenth- or nineteenth-century
edition could be considered later.

## Meaning of free

Project Gutenberg [states that its copyright determinations are U.S.-based](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html#us-only).
These older-edition leads reduce obvious recent-contributor issues but are not a
substitute for checking territorial rules and source terms. Candidate status must
never be interpreted as permission to publish an arbitrary edition with that title.
